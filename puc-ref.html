Portfolio category, by account

AI verdict (intrinsic, all 85)

Verdict rule: AA1 ≤ 2 → Not Justified; else AA2 ≤ 2 → Marginal; AA1 = 5 and AA2 ≥ 4 → Essential; otherwise Material Improvement. Scores: OV value, AA AI advantage, RR readiness (0–100; ND = coverage below 70%).

How categories are assigned
  1. Relevance screen (account line of business).
  2. Any gate Not Eligible → PC8.
  3. AI Not Justified → PC6.
  4. AI Marginal → PC5.
  5. Cannot Assess, an ND index or confidence Low or below → PC3 if OV ≥ 55, else PC7.
  6. Residual risk High → PC7.
  7. OV ≥ 55 and RR ≥ 55 → PC1.
  8. OV ≥ 55 → PC2.
  9. RR ≥ 55 → PC4.
  10. Otherwise → PC7.
